About Us

Why Choose Mobile Detailing?

Skip the drop-off hassle. We bring professional equipment directly to your driveway, office, or apartment complex in Colorado Springs. You save time, avoid coordinating rides, and get showroom results without leaving home. Schedule around your life while we handle the detail work.

Our Detailing Process

Booking: Call (719) 431-3749 or book online. We prefer 24 hours notice but often accommodate same-day requests.

What You Provide: Access to a power outlet within 100 feet of your vehicle. Remove valuables before we arrive. You can leave during service—just provide keys.

The Detail: Heavy-duty vacuum, targeted cleaning by package level, specialized pet hair removal tools, enzyme-based odor treatments, and brand-name products (Dawn, Resolve, no harsh chemicals). Times: Basic ~2hrs, Complete ~3hrs, Luxe 4-5hrs.

Colorado Springs Service Area

We serve Colorado Springs and surrounding areas within 30 miles of downtown, including Broadmoor, Stratmoor Hills, Southeast/East Colorado Springs, Briargate, Northgate, Monument, Black Forest, Fountain, and Security-Widefield. Custom pricing for areas beyond 30 miles. We detail at residential, apartment, and office locations.

Veteran-Owned & Operated

Invictus Auto Detailing is owned by an Army veteran (Captain, Field Artillery, 8 years service) with deployments to CENTCOM and EURCOM, honorably discharged with a Bronze Star. Military discipline translates to precision detailing—attention to detail, commitment to excellence, no shortcuts.

Our Guarantee: Unsatisfied with any aspect? We'll make it right at no charge. Military, first responders, and seniors 65+ receive 10% off with valid ID.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve in Colorado Springs?

We provide mobile interior detailing throughout Colorado Springs and surrounding areas within 30 miles of downtown, including Broadmoor, Stratmoor Hills, Southeast Colorado Springs, East Colorado Springs, Briargate, Northgate, Monument, Black Forest, Fountain, and Security-Widefield. Custom pricing applies for areas beyond 30 miles.

How long does an interior detail take?

Our Basic package takes approximately 2 hours, Complete package around 3 hours, and Luxe package 4-5 hours. Times may vary by 30 minutes depending on vehicle size and condition. Add-on services may extend the time needed.

Do I need to be present during the detail?

No, you can leave during the service. Just provide your keys and access to a power outlet within 100 feet of your vehicle. We'll do a walkthrough together when the job is complete.

What do I need to provide?

You only need to provide access to a standard power outlet within 100 feet of your vehicle. We bring all equipment, supplies, and cleaning products. Please remove valuables, personal items, and anything you don't want moved before we arrive.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards, Venmo, and Zelle. Payment is due upon completion of service. We offer 10% discounts for military, first responders, and seniors 65+ with valid ID.

Can you remove pet hair and odors?

Absolutely! We use specialized tools designed specifically for pet hair removal without damaging carpet fibers. For odors, we use enzyme-based treatments that eliminate sources rather than just masking smells. Our Deep Odor Treatment add-on can handle even the toughest smoke, pet, and mildew odors.

How far in advance should I book?

We prefer 24 hours notice, but we can often accommodate same-day requests. A rush fee may apply for last-minute bookings. Call (719) 431-3749 to check availability.

What about large vehicles like SUVs and trucks?

We detail all vehicle sizes. Large vehicles (SUVs, 3-row vehicles) have a $25 surcharge. Vehicles with 4+ rows require custom pricing. All our packages and add-ons are available regardless of vehicle size.
